# Cleanup, History, and Future

> **A Tolerated Ambiguity Becomes a Copied Convention.** Clarify, isolate, lower, merge, or remove misleading knowledge before future Agents learn it as the local norm.

Documentation cleanup is semantic work. The goal is not a tidy tree by itself; it is to remove misleading authority and preserve the minimum knowledge that still changes future decisions.

## Available actions

### Keep current

The material remains the Current Home and its routes are healthy.

### Clarify

The content remains useful but its scope, authority, or freshness is ambiguous.

### Merge

Several surfaces express one meaning and can be combined without losing independent ownership or history.

### Move

The meaning belongs to another semantic home. Preserve inbound routes when readers still arrive from the old location.

### Lower

Retain as source input, evidence, working material, future candidate, or history rather than current authority.

### Delete

Remove when the material is duplicated, misleading, obsolete, and no longer explains a current constraint or decision.

## History

Keep history when it helps answer:

```text
why the current shape exists
which option was rejected and why
what constraint used to apply
how a migration or incident unfolded
what evidence remains useful
```

Do not keep history merely because deletion feels risky. Version control already preserves file history; a separate historical surface is useful only when future readers need an intentional route.

## Future knowledge

Distinguish:

```text
accepted target     a chosen future direction not yet current
candidate proposal  an option still awaiting acceptance
research input      evidence or exploration that may influence a decision
```

Do not create a future copy of every Current Home. Prefer capability-oriented proposals or roadmap entries that link to the current owner and state explicit promotion conditions.

## Broken windows

A clearly wrong document should be repaired, lowered, isolated, or deleted. A permanent warning banner with no owner or resolution condition normalizes ambiguity.

If immediate repair is impossible, contain the damage:

```text
name the conflict
link to the current owner
state what must not be relied upon
state what evidence or decision resolves it
```

## Flattening and retirement

When a partition, home, identifier, or template no longer earns its cost, flatten or retire it. Convergence is reversible; documentation shape is not a maturity ladder.
