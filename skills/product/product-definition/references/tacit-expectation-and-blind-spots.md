# Tacit Expectations and Blind Spots

Some important product expectations are unknown to the team until a concrete
alternative makes them recognizable. Do not force every uncertainty into a
verbal questionnaire.

## Elicitation Surfaces

```text
contrasting low-fidelity prototypes
reference products or real cases
opposite behavior examples
state / permission / exception matrices
before-and-after stories
ranked alternatives with tradeoffs
small reversible experiments
```

Ask what feels wrong and why. The reaction is source material, not automatically
an accepted decision.

## Blind-spot Pass

Challenge the current model with:

```text
missing actor or responsibility
missing object or lifecycle
happy-path bias
exception, retry, timeout, cancellation, expiry, and recovery
concurrency and delegation
permission and data-visibility conflict
migration from current behavior
metric gaming or ambiguous time basis
unknown operational or policy dependency
future behavior accidentally implied by current source
```

## Ownership

A discovered expectation becomes:

```text
source-derived observation
recommended interpretation
decision packet when material
accepted product decision only after the accountable owner accepts it
```

Architecture and Harness work may expose the unknown but cannot decide product
meaning on behalf of Product Authority.
