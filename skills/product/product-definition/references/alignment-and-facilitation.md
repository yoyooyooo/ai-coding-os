# Alignment and Facilitation

Product alignment is a decision process, not a document-reading meeting. Prepare stakeholders to decide specific questions with visible options, impacts, and recommendations.

## Before the meeting

Define:

```text
meeting objective
version or capability in scope
decisions required
required decision owners
subject-matter participants
pre-read sources and product model
items that are informational only
items that can be resolved asynchronously
```

Do not invite a large group to discover basic source conflicts live if they can be synthesized beforehand.

## Alignment pack

For each decision item include:

```text
issue and why it matters
source claims or current behavior
recommended option
alternatives and tradeoffs
impact on users, operations, scope, workflow, state, rules, permissions, metrics, quality, and delivery
what happens if no decision is made
owner and needed-by date
```

Group items by decision owner or workflow, not merely by source document order.

## During the meeting

Use this sequence:

```text
confirm objective and decision authority
confirm shared definitions and version boundary
review the recommendation before debating alternatives
capture the final decision, conditions, and effective version
separate newly discovered issues from the current decision
assign unresolved items with owner and deadline
confirm which product artifacts will be updated
```

Do not let the meeting drift into technical implementation detail unless that detail changes product behavior or reveals a feasibility decision.

## Decision states

```text
accepted
accepted with conditions
rejected
needs more evidence
needs another authority
blocked by dependency
deferred to a named version
```

“Discussed” and “generally aligned” are not decision states.

## After the meeting

Within the project’s normal decision latency:

```text
update PDRs or accepted decision records
update scope baseline, product model, workflow, rules, permissions, metrics, PRDs, and acceptance as affected
mark superseded recommendations or assumptions
record unresolved issues in the clarification register
send concise closure notes with links to updated authorities
```

The meeting pack and minutes are views and evidence of discussion. They should not become a competing product SSoT.

## Asynchronous alignment

For distributed teams, a decision packet can be reviewed asynchronously when it contains:

```text
clear decision statement
explicit deadline and owner
recommended option
structured response choices
impact and residual risk
rule for resolving no-response or conflicting responses
```

Do not interpret silence as approval unless the organization has an explicit and accepted rule allowing it.
