# Product Reframing Examples

Examples are diagnostic patterns, not templates to copy blindly.

## "Export to Excel"

### Initial request

"Add a daily Excel export."

### Reframed outcome

Regional owners need a reliable way to receive exception cases, acknowledge ownership, and track resolution.

### Product implications

```text
assignment and visibility
exception lifecycle
notification and acknowledgement
resolution evidence
reporting as a secondary representation
```

An export may still be useful, but it is no longer mistaken for the whole product need.

## "Add an AI reviewer"

### Initial request

"Let an Agent approve low-risk cases automatically."

### Reframed outcome

Reduce review time without allowing uncertain model output to become an accepted decision without the required evidence and authority.

### Product implications

```text
candidate recommendation versus accepted decision
confidence and evidence visibility
permission to accept or override
appeal and audit
failure and no-decision behavior
```

## "Make a dashboard"

### Initial request

"Build a dashboard for management."

### Reframed outcome

Management needs to detect delayed work, identify ownership, and intervene before SLA failure.

### Product implications

The core capability may be a work-state model, escalation policy, and notification path. Charts are one surface.

## "Clone the prototype"

### Initial request

"The AI prototype already works; productionize it."

### Reframed investigation

```text
which behavior is accepted product meaning
which data and providers are fake
which code is disposable Prototype material
which path can become a retained Tracer
who owns facts, permissions, recovery, and observability
```

## "Unify all roles"

### Initial request

"Map every Audit role one-to-one to an Inspection role."

### Reframed problem

Determine whether the systems need shared identity and permission primitives or truly share business roles. Preserve distinct domain roles when their responsibilities and lifecycle differ.
