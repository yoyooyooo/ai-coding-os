## Evidence handoff notes

Delivery evidence links may point to a Harness Result, selected execution-method evidence, Docs Evidence classification, or a shared Evidence Envelope. Preserve the source claim ceiling. A passing Harness or completed workflow item does not make the product delivered; verified current behavior does not become accepted future intent.

## Identifier notes

Use stable project-specific IDs. The optional scanner resolves explicit `@ID` references and duplicate definitions; it does not judge semantic coverage.
