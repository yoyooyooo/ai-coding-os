# Semantic Compression

## Purpose

高熵词汇把一条长因果链压缩成能够在现场迅速唤醒判断的短语。它不是文学装饰，也不是中央私有 DSL。好的词汇既可被记住，又能在正确节点被完整解压。

## Compression contract

每个 canonical anchor 必须同时具有：

```text
stable exact spelling
one compressed judgment
formal meaning
clear contrast or boundary
one realistic consequence or example
one semantic Owner
```

只有短句没有解压会形成口号；只有长解释没有短句会增加恢复成本。

## Network-level anchors

全套共同使用的网络级短句限制在少量稳定集合：

```text
Project Authority First.
Source Is Not Decision.
Evidence Bounds Claims.
One Scoped Meaning, One Current Home.
Route Is an Edge, Not a Sequence.
Local Agency, Bounded Authority.
No Silent Material Assumption.
Strong Invariants, Weak Choreography.
Minimal Context, Maximal Legibility.
Shape Must Be Earned.
Portable Defaults Standardize the Boring Choices.
The Project Should Explain Itself.
Feedback Horizon Sets the Safe Step Size.
One Failure, One Lowest Reliable Owner.
Preserve Semantics; Re-earn Scaffolding and Conventions.
```

每个 Specialist 额外保留约 3–5 个局部 anchor。不要为每篇 Reference 发明新的格言。

## Mnemonic and formal term

允许记忆句与正式术语成对存在：

```text
One Fact, One Final Writer.
  -> Final Materialization Authority

The Project Should Explain Itself.
  -> Agent-Legible Change Surface

Prototype Learns; Tracer Grows.
  -> Work Intent distinction

Do Not Outrun Your Headlights.
  -> Feedback Horizon
```

记忆句帮助现场判断，正式术语帮助精确讨论和检索。

## Selective pragmatic metaphors

经典高熵词只在它能稳定唤醒当前因果链时保留：

```text
ETC / Easier to Change        changeability compass, not a universal score
DRY                           one knowledge authority, not zero textual repetition
Orthogonality                 keep independent change axes independent
Reversibility                 preserve the ability to find, isolate, replace, migrate, and recover
Broken Windows                a tolerated ambiguity becomes a copied convention
Tracer Bullet                 a thin real path that remains and grows
Do Not Outrun Your Headlights Feedback Horizon sets the safe step size
Cargo Cult / Coconut Airport  visible form without the mechanism that made it work
```

这些词不应全部升级为根级口号；它们进入能够真正改变判断的 Owner。

## Repetition and DRY

重要 anchor 可以在根地图、Owner 主 Skill 和一个关键 Reference 中精确复现。这里的重复是必要上下文，不是多个平级 Authority。详细定义仍由一个 Owner 持有。

## Production identifiers

高熵隐喻用于原则和认知工具，不应污染生产代码命名。代码、路径、API 和 Schema 需要准确、可搜索、一致，不使用 `broken-window-manager`, `tracer-bullet-service` 等文学化标识，除非它本身就是领域术语。

## Admission and retirement

新增 anchor 前先问：

```text
它是否压缩一个反复出现、会改变下一步判断的因果链？
是否已有稳定词能够承载？
它属于哪个 Owner？
是否有明确反面和边界？
加入后会减少还是增加 Suite 私有方言？
```

模型和项目能力变化后，保留仍能改变判断的 anchor；合并同义词，删除只剩风格价值的短句。
